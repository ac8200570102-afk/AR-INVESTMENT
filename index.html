Mutual Funds

Understand different types of Mutual Funds

📈

Equity Mutual Funds

Equity funds primarily invest in shares of companies. They can be suitable for long-term investors who can tolerate market fluctuations.

🏦

Debt Mutual Funds

Debt funds invest mainly in fixed-income securities such as bonds and money-market instruments.

⚖️

Hybrid Mutual Funds

Hybrid funds combine equity and debt investments according to the scheme's investment strategy.

🌱

Small Cap Funds

These funds invest primarily in smaller companies and may offer higher growth potential along with higher volatility.

🚀

Mid Cap Funds

Mid-cap funds invest mainly in medium-sized companies and are generally considered between large-cap and small-cap funds in terms of risk.

🏆

Large Cap Funds

Large-cap funds primarily invest in established large companies and may offer relatively lower volatility than small-cap funds.

Investment Learning

Learn the basics before you invest

💡

What is SIP?

SIP allows investors to invest a fixed amount periodically in a mutual fund scheme.

📊

What is NAV?

NAV represents the per-unit value of a mutual fund scheme's assets after accounting for liabilities.

🎯

Goal Based Investing

Investing should be connected with financial goals such as education, home purchase or retirement.

⏳

Power of Compounding

Staying invested for a longer period can allow returns to generate further returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Mutual Fund?

A mutual fund pools money from multiple investors and invests it according to the scheme's objective.

Is SIP an investment product?

SIP is a method of investing a fixed amount periodically into a mutual fund scheme.

Can Mutual Funds give guaranteed returns?

No. Mutual fund returns are generally market-linked and are not guaranteed.

What is the minimum amount required for SIP?

The minimum SIP amount depends on the mutual fund scheme and platform through which the investment is made.
